# Problem statement

A friend recommends *Dune*. Later, the viewer remembers the title but can't remember whether the recommendation was for the 1984 film or the 2021 film. They want to see the year, recognize the poster, read a short description, and save the right film before they forget. In my experience, that quick lookup on IMDb can feel slow and dated. The few details I came for can take several taps to find.

Movie Hub will put those details together on one search result card. A viewer can compare films with the same title and save one directly to a private watchlist. When they return, they can see what they meant to watch, mark a film watched, or remove it.

The first release will use a movie metadata service for discovery and a local application database for each viewer's choices. Its success test is simple: a viewer searching for *Dune* can identify and save the intended release from the results, then find it again on their next visit.
